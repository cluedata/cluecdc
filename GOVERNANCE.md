# Governance

ClueCDC uses a maintainer-led governance model during the `0.x` phase.
Maintainers review changes, triage releases and security work, and are accountable
for technical direction and community health.

Decisions favor the standalone Community Edition, backward compatibility,
security, and operational simplicity. Significant changes should begin with a
public design issue and record alternatives and migration impact. Routine changes
are decided through pull-request review; unresolved architectural decisions are
decided by maintainer consensus, with the project owner as tie-breaker.

Consistent contributors may be invited as reviewers or maintainers based on
technical judgment, constructive participation, and sustained responsibility.
Maintainer additions and removals must be recorded publicly. A future commercial
extension or hosted service may depend on Community interfaces, but may not make
the Community Edition require a commercial service to run.
