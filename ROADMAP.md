# Roadmap

These are ongoing priorities, not promises of delivery dates or current features.
See [the feature matrix](docs/features.md) for implemented capabilities and limits.

- Expand real CDC CRUD, lifecycle, replay, outage/recovery, and upgrade verification.
- Improve Data Quality comparability, provider coverage, scheduling, and evidence.
- Improve CDC Observability coverage, trusted exporter integration, and diagnostics.
- Qualify experimental sources and warehouse destinations against real services.
- Add production identity/SSO and external secret integrations.
- Strengthen high-availability guidance, least-privilege deployment, backup, and recovery.
- Keep local setup, shared demo/test infrastructure, and documentation maintainable.

Propose priorities through [contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) and
[governance](GOVERNANCE.md). Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
